# White Balance 3.1.0

The panel gains a preset menu, a better pair of sliders and an as-shot line. The
stage is unchanged: its text, and so its frozen hash, stay as they are.

## What kind of photo is open

Core bakes the camera's as-shot white balance into the decoded pixels. The Temp
slider is a Kelvin value measured against the photo's as-shot Kelvin
(`exif.colorTemperature`). Tint is a green shift relative to as-shot.

| Kind | Rule | As-shot Kelvin comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Camera RAW | `exif.colorTemperature` set, not a `.dng` | the camera's own multipliers, through core's blackbody fit |
| DNG | `exif.colorTemperature` set, a `.dng` | the DNG's colour matrices (a real CCT) |
| Rendered | no `exif.colorTemperature` (JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, or a RAW whose as-shot could not be read) | nothing; core uses 6500 K |

## Preset menu

One menu above the sliders. Choosing an entry writes Temp and Tint and makes
one undo step named `White Balance: <entry>`. Auto runs core's Auto, which
makes its own step.

- Every kind: **As Shot** (Temp = as-shot Kelvin, Tint 0), **Auto**, **Custom**
  (shown only, never chosen).
- Camera RAW whose body is in the table: that camera's own presets, in the
  camera's menu order, under a heading with the camera's name.
- Camera RAW whose body is not in the table: one disabled line, "No presets for
  this camera". Cameras disagree too much for one generic Daylight to be right:
  across the table, Daylight lands anywhere from 4720 K to 13610 K on this scale.
- DNG: standard presets on the CCT scale: Daylight 5500 K, Cloudy 6500 K,
  Shade 7500 K, Tungsten 2850 K, Fluorescent 3800 K / Tint +16, Flash 5500 K.
- Rendered: no light-source presets. A JPEG's colours are already processed.

The menu shows the entry the current Temp and Tint equal: As Shot, a preset, or
Auto when they equal what Auto last set on this photo in this session.
Anything else is Custom.

### Camera presets

The table is darktable's `data/wb_presets.json` (GPL-3.0-or-later), generated
into `src/camera-presets.data.ts` by `scripts/camera-presets.mjs`: per camera,
the multipliers its raws are balanced with for each preset at fine-tuning 0.

- **Temp** is core's `kelvinFromWhiteBalanceGains` of the preset's multipliers,
  the same fit core used for the photo's as-shot Kelvin. A preset whose
  multipliers equal the as-shot ones lands exactly on As Shot.
- **Tint** is the green the preset asks for beyond what the blackbody fit
  predicts between the photo's as-shot multipliers (`exif.asShotMultipliers`,
  Safelight 3.2.5) and the preset's. A preset equal to the as-shot multipliers
  is Tint 0, and a shot taken under fluorescent light gets a green-ward Tint
  for Daylight.
- Without as-shot multipliers (an older Safelight, or a photo imported before
  3.2.5 and not yet opened in Develop), Tint is measured from the camera's
  Daylight preset (or Direct Sunlight, or Sunny) instead. A camera with none of
  those then gets Tint 0 on every preset.

Matching: the EXIF make picks a family (Canon, Fujifilm, Kodak, Leaf, Leica,
Minolta, Nikon, Olympus with OM System, Panasonic, Pentax with Ricoh, Phase
One, Samsung, Sony). The model must match a table model in the same family once
both are reduced to lowercase letters and digits, with a leading maker name
dropped ("NIKON Z 6_2" and "Z 6_2" are both `z62`). A Canon body's Rebel or
Kiss name ("Canon EOS REBEL T2i", "Canon EOS Kiss X4") finds the row of its EOS
name ("EOS 550D"): `src/camera-aliases.data.ts`, generated by
`scripts/camera-aliases.mjs` from rawspeed's `data/cameras.xml`.

## Sliders

- Plain tracks, like every other Develop slider, on the panel and the mask
  instance.
- Shift+double-click either slider runs Auto (core `onAuto`, Safelight 3.2.5;
  older builds reset instead).
- Camera RAW and DNG: Temp in Kelvin, 2000 to 50000, as before.
- Rendered: Temp is relative, −100 to +100, 0 at the as-shot (6500 K). One
  step is one mired: `K = 10⁶ / (10⁶/asShot − v)`, so +100 is about 18600 K
  and −100 about 3940 K. The stored value is still Kelvin.
- Tint is unchanged, −150 to +150.

## As-shot line

Under the sliders, in secondary text:

- Camera RAW and DNG: `As shot 7350 K`, then ` · <EXIF white balance>` when
  the EXIF has one ("Auto", "Daylight", "Manual").
- Rendered: `As shot · <EXIF white balance>` when the EXIF has one, else
  nothing.

## Unchanged

Picker, the mask instance's ranges, the reset, the panel id, the stage.
